LETTER FROM THE EDITOR • ISSUE 01

Keeping Our Hands on the Wheel

What are you looking at? You’re reading the first issue of Ctrl + Care, a DIY digital zine created for class to explore what work looks like in the age of AI. This project started with one practical question: as students preparing to enter our careers, how do we actually thrive alongside new technology instead of becoming overpowered by it?

Editor’s Note: Although this project was originally assigned as a group zine, there was no communication/collaboration from my assigned group members. Rather than leave the project unfinished, I took it upon myself to build the website, design the layout, and complete this issue solo—focusing specifically on my chosen field, pharmacy. I am aware that the purpose is to work as a group, however, I decided to see this as a challenge to see how much I can learn along the way.

Putting a web zine together from scratch wasn’t smooth. Between learning how to code and host pages on GitHub, sorting through unnecesary internet hype, and separating real workplace changes from unplausible sci-fi possibilities, I realized something important: using AI to help build a project doesn’t replace the human work of deciding what matters, fact-checking sources, and giving something a real voice.

The Core Thread: Behind the pharmacy counter, and across modern healthcare, one truth keeps standing out. AI is great at absorbing repetitive functions, such as paperwork and sorting data, but it cannot hold ethical responsibility, read a worried patient’s face, or build genuine human trust with an individual.

Think of Ctrl + Care as a pocket survival guide for the next few years. It isn’t here to tell you to fear technology or to worship it, rather, it’s here to endorse human intelligence, safety, and care in a fast-changing world.

— Stephanie Enriquez
